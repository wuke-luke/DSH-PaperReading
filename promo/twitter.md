## Single · English

Found a neat DSH (DeepSeek Harness) plugin for reading papers — DSH-PaperReading.

Bilingual page pairs (translated left, original right), select-to-translate, and a paper-aware chat whose answers cite pages you can click.

Open source, MIT, runs locally.
github.com/wuke-luke/DSH-PaperReading

## Thread（推荐，3 条）

**1/**
Reading an English paper usually means four windows: the PDF, a translator, a chat window, and the notes app.

Found a DSH plugin that puts all four in one sidebar — DSH-PaperReading 🧵

**2/**
What it actually does:

• imports local PDF / arXiv ID / arXiv link
• page-by-page bilingual view (translated page ⇄ original)
• drag over a sentence → translation appears next to the cursor
• paper chat that cites [p.12] and jumps there
• notes, diagrams, sync, per-paper token usage

**3/**
The part I like: layout follows the paper — two-column text, figures and footnotes stay in place, and text inside figures is left alone instead of being mangled.

Everything (PDFs, translations, notes) stays on your machine. MIT.
github.com/wuke-luke/DSH-PaperReading
